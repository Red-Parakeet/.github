About

Red Parakeet is an offensive security collective specializing in red teaming, adversarial simulation, and security research, and building the open-source tools to support them.

We were founded on a simple premise: the security community deserves better tooling than proprietary black boxes and paywalled research allow. What began as an independent research effort has grown into a collective that combines hands-on offensive engagements with the open-source tools our own operators rely on in the field. Every tool we release reflects the same standard we hold our client engagements to.

Services

In addition to our open-source work, Red Parakeet provides professional offensive security services for organizations looking to validate and strengthen their defenses:

Penetration Testing — Structured assessments of networks, applications, and infrastructure to identify and validate exploitable vulnerabilities, delivered with clear, actionable remediation guidance.
Red Team Engagements — Full-scope adversarial simulations that mirror the tactics, techniques, and procedures of real-world threat actors, testing people, process, and technology together rather than in isolation.
Social Engineering & Physical Assessments — Vishing, phishing/smishing campaigns, and physical intrusion testing to evaluate human and facility-level security controls.
Hardware & IoT Security Assessments — Evaluation of embedded systems, HID devices, and connected hardware for exploitable weaknesses.

For engagement inquiries, reach out at contact@redparakeet.org.
